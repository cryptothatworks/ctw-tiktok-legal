Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Crypto That Works TikTok Uploader" application (the "App"), a private, single-user automation tool that publishes the Crypto That Works channel's own video content to its own TikTok account.

Who uses this app

The App has exactly one user: its developer/owner. It is not distributed to, or used by, anyone else, and does not have a sign-up flow, login page, or public interface of any kind.

What data the app accesses

Using TikTok's official OAuth flow, the App requests the user.info.profile and video.publish scopes, solely to authenticate as the owner's own TikTok account and upload video files on their behalf. The App does not access, read, or collect data belonging to any other TikTok user, and does not access followers, comments, direct messages, or analytics belonging to any account other than the owner's own.

What data is stored

The App stores, locally on the owner's own machine (never on a shared or third-party server): an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok for the owner's own account, and the video files being uploaded (which the owner created themselves). No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok's own account settings (Settings -> Manage account -> Apps), which immediately invalidates the stored tokens. Deleting the locally stored token file has the same effect from the App's side.

Cookies and tracking

The App has no web front-end, does not use cookies, and does not track or profile any user.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time as the tool evolves.

Contact

Questions about this App can be sent to cryptothatworks@gmail.com.
