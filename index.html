Crypto That Works TikTok Uploader

This is an internal, single-user automation tool used by the Crypto That Works YouTube channel to publish its own pre-produced video content (Shorts) to its own TikTok account, via TikTok's official Content Posting API.

It is not a public product or service - it has exactly one user (its developer/owner), no sign-up flow, and no public interface.
